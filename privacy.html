Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Information we use

We use account email, name, phone number, delivery address, cart and order details to provide the store, fulfil orders, prevent fraud and offer customer support.

Authentication and storage

Account passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in browser localStorage or Google Sheets. Product data and orders use Firebase services. Product backup records are mirrored to the linked Google Sheet. Product images may be stored in Firebase Storage and backed up to Google Drive.

Notifications

Order email is sent to the email used for the account. Telegram messages are sent only after the customer explicitly links Telegram by starting the store bot; a bot cannot identify a Telegram account from a phone number alone.

Sharing

We use service providers required to operate the store, including Firebase, Vercel, Google, Resend, Telegram and payment providers. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and choices

Order records may be retained for fulfilment, support and legal purposes. Contact the store to request correction or deletion where applicable. Safety backups may retain archived records for recovery and audit.
